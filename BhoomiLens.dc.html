Design notes

Design system, gap-to-feature map, and the assumptions taken where the brief was open.

Design system

{{ s.name }}
{{ s.hex }}
  • Type: Noto Sans with Noto Sans Devanagari, 16px minimum body, 1.5 line height, monospace only for identifiers.
  • 8-point spacing scale, 8px radius, two shadow levels only.
  • Saffron is used for fills and the persistent disclaimer band, never as text colour.
  • Risk is always icon plus text plus colour: ■ high, ▲ medium, ✓ clear.
  • Dark mode is a token swap on the same variables; contrast holds at AA in both modes.
  • Responsive at 1440, 768 and 390; all targets at least 48px.

Search, indexing and privacy

  • Clean routes: /, /parcel/{ULPIN}, /cases/{id}, /objection, /compensation, /upload, /mediation, /notifications, /officer/*, /help/faq. The bar under the header shows the live route and its index policy.
  • Only public information pages are indexable. Parcel, case, objection, compensation, notification and officer pages carry a robots noindex directive, set live as you navigate — owner data must never be indexed (Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023).
  • Structured data: GovernmentService on home, FAQPage on the FAQ block, BreadcrumbList throughout; injected per route.
  • One h1 per screen, ordered h2 and h3, semantic header, nav, main, section, article and footer.
  • Language structure is hreflang-ready: a language code per route for 22 scheduled languages, machine translation through the national translation service, with human-reviewed legal strings.
  • Maps are lazily loaded and drawn as vectors, so the largest content paint stays text-first.
